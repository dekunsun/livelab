## Reading, against the rules fixed before the rerun

*The tables above are generated and unedited. This section was written after the results were
known.*

**The headline survives, and my explanation of the false alerts does not.**

### Abstention: identical under both legends

Abstention on Blind is **0, 4 and 3 of 31** under the corrected description, exactly as under the
wrong one, for all three models. By the registered reading, *the real-plant replication survives
its own harness error*. Pv1 is met for Gemini and Astra. Opus's 13% is 4 items against a bar of
3.1, the same margin as in the original study, and under the three-item rule it is not
interpreted.

### False alerts: the correction's predicted effect did not happen

I predicted (Pv2, Pv4) that correcting the description would remove most of Opus's and Astra's
false alerts, because they cited the misdescribed heaters. It did not. Opus went from 12 to 13 and
Astra from 37 to 36, and every remaining alert still cites a heater. What changed is the
argument. Told correctly that T701 is heater H701, the models now say:

- Opus: *"Heater H701 temperature climbs steadily 389→396 °C while reboiler T703 stays flat at
  ~52.8 °C, indicating heat is not transferring to the liquid."*
- Astra: *"H701 reads 396.233 °C, markedly above H702 at 63.667 °C and the reboiler at 58.78 °C."*

These are coherent physical arguments, and the plant's experts marked every one of these runs
fault-free. So the alerts are the models' judgment of what is normal for *this* plant, not a
misreading the harness planted. By the registered reading for Pv2's failure, **the false-alert
rate says something about the models on real data**, and is reported as such:

| | Gemini 3.8 Live | Claude Opus 5 | GPT-6 Astra |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fault-free runs called ANOMALOUS, corrected description | 3/37 (8%) | 13/37 (35%) | 36/37 (97%) |

### Gemini moved, and not for the reason predicted

Gemini's false alerts fell from 8 to 3 (Pv3 failed: it was predicted not to move). Its detection
on Full fell with them, from 20 to 10 of 31. None of its V1 alerts cited a heater, so this is not
the heater correction at work. The legend changed four things at once, including reflux and
distillate, and this study cannot say which one Gemini responded to. It became more willing to
call a run normal, whether the run was faulty or not.

### What this does to the correction published this morning

The correction said the legend was wrong, and it was. It also said the false-alert rates were
"at least partly the cost of a wrong description". For Opus 5 and Astra that is now measured and
**not supported**. For Gemini, the rates moved, but not through the channel the correction named.
The correction is amended in the registration's deviation log and in the README.
