## Reading, against the rules fixed before the run

*The tables above are generated and unedited. This section was written after the results were
known.*

**The result does not match any row of the registered reading table exactly, so by that table it
is reported as observed, with no mechanism claimed beyond what the records show.**

On fault-free runs, counted in items out of 29:

| What changed from the telemetry arm | Extra false alerts |
| --- | ---: |
| The sentence announcing photographs, nothing attached | **6** |
| The sentence, plus photographs of a **different** normal run | **10** |
| The sentence, plus **this run's** photographs | **11** |

- **About half of the shift comes from the words alone.** The sentence with no photographs
  produced 6 of the 11 extra false alerts.
- **The rest comes from photographs being attached, not from what they show.** Footage of a
  different run gets to 10. This run's own footage adds 1 more item, which is below the three-item
  difference this project does not interpret.
- **Every false alert on a fault-free run, in all four arms, cites the same reading:** `T701`, the
  reboiler temperature (9, 20, 15 and 19 of 29 alerts respectively). The camera cue does not bring
  in new evidence. It moves a borderline reading of one channel over the line.
- **Opus never noticed the photographs were missing.** In the sentence arm it was told six
  photographs were attached and none were. None of its 48 answers mentions photographs at all:
  none claims to have seen them, and none says they are absent. The committed request confirms
  that none were sent. This is the project's central finding in a new place: evidence that should
  be there and is not goes unremarked, and the verdict moves anyway.

Blind items move the same way but by 2 to 4 items per arm (+21, +21, +11 points). Differences
between those arms are not interpreted.

### The predictions

- **Pf1 (the sentence does less than half): not met, on the line.** The bar was +19 points and the
  sentence arm moved +21: 6 items against a bar of 5.5. It is not met as written. It does not
  support reading the sentence as either negligible or the whole story.
- **Pf2 (another run's photographs do as much): met.** +34 against +38, one item apart.
- **Pf3 (abstention ≤ 10%): not met by one item.** The sentence arm abstained on 2 of 19 blind
  items, the same count as the telemetry arm. This is below the three-item rule, and nothing is
  read into it.

### What it means

For a lab adding a camera beside its instruments, with this model, the camera **adds alarm, not
information**. What the camera showed made no measurable difference. Half the effect needs no
photograph at all, only the statement that one exists. The other half needs a photograph, any
photograph.

Rewording the prompt would therefore remove at most half the problem. The rest comes from
attaching images at all, which is the thing a camera integration does.

### What this cannot show

- **One model, one plant, one sentence, one run per item.**
- **Why `T701` in particular: answered, and it is this project's error.** `T701` is the
  temperature of heater H701 at the reboiler, and 300 to 500 °C is ordinary for a heater. The
  plant description the model was given called it a column temperature at the reboiler end,
  where 330 °C *would* mean a broken sensor. So the reading was borderline because the harness
  made it look implausible ([deviation 5](../realdata_preregistration.md)). The comparison between
  arms still holds, since every arm had the same description. What it shows is how a camera cue
  moves a model on a reading it already doubts, not how often a real plant looks faulty.
- **Donor footage is normal-run only**, as registered.
