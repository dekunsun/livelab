# Image attribution

Every image in `data/images/cvd/` is a panel cropped from a figure in an open-access article
published under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The only change is
cropping to one panel, excluding panel letters, overlaid parameters, scale-bar text and author
annotations. Per-image source, figure and panel are in `manifest.csv`.
